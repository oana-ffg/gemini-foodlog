# Gemini FoodLog project instructions

## Confirmed product concept

- A microcontroller camera points at the stove or counter and sends periodic images for analysis.
- People must change absolutely nothing about their habits: no staging ingredients, scanning items, photographing plates, or otherwise performing for the system.
- Use relevant inbound Nemlig recipe emails to help ground image interpretation in food that is actually available.
- The system asks questions when needed, learns from the answers, and logs food.
- The primary goal is a food journal for monitoring symptoms and investigating possible food triggers. Calorie tracking is secondary, not the defining purpose.

## Confirmed hackathon constraints

This is a new project for Google's 2026 [All Things Agentic Hackathon](https://allthingsagentichackathon.devpost.com/). The [official rules](https://allthingsagentichackathon.devpost.com/rules) are authoritative.

- The project must be newly created during the contest submission period.
- It must use Gemini 3.5 or newer through the Gemini API or Vertex AI.
- It must use at least one Google agent framework: Google ADK, GenAI SDK, Antigravity SDK, or Genkit.
- It must use at least one Google Cloud infrastructure service.
